VaultBeat is a modern, privacy-focused music player for Android.

Manage your local music library with ease, create custom playlists, and enjoy high-quality playback of your audio files. Built with Jetpack Compose, VaultBeat offers a sleek interface and powerful features without compromising your data privacy.

Main Features:
- Local library scanning and playback.
- Artist and Album organization.
- Custom Playlist creation and management.
- Fast and intuitive search.
- Clean and modern Material 3 design.

Your music, your device, your way.
